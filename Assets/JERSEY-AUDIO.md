Short WAVs retain their original pitch, duration and wording. Constant gain balances them to -20 LUFS, measured over four repetitions because each is shorter than two seconds. Playback uses the existing master volume. See [JERSEY-LEVELS.json](JERSEY-LEVELS.json) for durations, gains, measured loudness and true peaks.

Only one optional reaction may play every 45 seconds. Ambient smiles have a 25% chance, disappointment 35%, and idle side-eye 15%. Mirror and gift reactions are deliberate cues but share the cooldown. Reactions are silent during notes, tutorial, focus, Spotify listening, pause and unavailable interactions. They yield to existing sounds and dance music; original head-pet, coffee, crossed-arm, tumble, clap and shutdown mappings remain in place. Muting stops all clips. There is no Jersey dance.
